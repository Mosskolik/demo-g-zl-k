# Angelina veri seti için GitHub mesajı (issue)

**Title (başlık):**
License / permission to use the dataset for a non-commercial student accessibility project

**Mesaj (kutuya yapıştır):**

Hello, and thank you for Angelina Braille Reader and this dataset.

We are a high school team (Team Orbital, Türkiye) building a free, voice-controlled math assistant for blind and visually impaired students, for the TEKNOFEST "Technology for the Benefit of Humanity" competition. We would like to add reading of embossed Turkish Braille from phone photos.

We could not find a license file in this repository. Could you tell us under which terms the dataset may be used? Specifically, may we:

1. use the images and annotations to train and evaluate our own small Braille cell detection model, and
2. use the resulting model in our free, non-commercial app?

We will not redistribute the images, and we will credit you and the dataset (link and paper citation) in our app and competition report. We also sent the same question to admin@angelina-reader.com.

Thank you!
